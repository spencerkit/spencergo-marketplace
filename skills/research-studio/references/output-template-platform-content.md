# Platform / Content Ecosystem Output Template

Use when the topic is mainly about a content platform, traffic ecosystem, audience appetite, or content opportunity.

## Default compact structure

### 结论
One-line judgment on the platform/content opportunity.

### 核心发现
- audience appetite
- supply density
- current winning pattern

### 多维度结果
- 平台特征：what matters on this platform now
- 用户偏好：what audiences currently seem to respond to
- 内容供给：how crowded the field is
- 分发逻辑：how visibility is likely won or lost
- 变现逻辑：how creators or operators capture value
- 风险：where the content direction may underperform
- 机会：what angle still feels underfilled

### 最大机会
Name the best opening.

### 最大风险
Name the strongest platform-fit or saturation risk.

### 建议打法
Suggest how to approach the platform or content angle.

### 信息缺口
What still needs validation.

## What to emphasize

Emphasize:
- audience appetite
- supply saturation
- distribution logic
- platform fit
- realistic content angle

Do not write empty platform buzzwords.
