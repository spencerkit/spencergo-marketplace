# Analysis Dimensions

## 1. Principle

Choose dimensions based on topic type.
Do not apply every possible dimension to every topic.
Use the smallest useful set that still gives a rounded judgment.

---

## 2. General-purpose dimensions

Use these often:
- definition and scope
- current state
- demand / audience
- competition / supply
- monetization / business model
- growth drivers
- risks / uncertainty
- opportunities
- conclusion

---

## 3. Market / industry topics

Recommended dimensions:
- market definition and scope
- current market state
- key players
- demand drivers
- competition pattern
- regulation or structural constraints
- monetization and value capture
- opportunities
- major risks

---

## 4. Product / project topics

Recommended dimensions:
- problem being solved
- target users
- existing alternatives
- differentiation difficulty
- monetization path
- execution difficulty
- acquisition / distribution challenges
- risks
- opportunity window

---

## 5. Platform / content ecosystem topics

Recommended dimensions:
- platform characteristics
- audience appetite
- content supply density
- current winning patterns
- monetization logic
- discoverability / distribution
- saturation risk
- opportunity gap

---

## 6. Concept / trend topics

Recommended dimensions:
- definition and boundary
- why the topic is rising now
- current adoption state
- main narratives around it
- what is hype vs what has evidence
- likely short-term direction
- risks of overestimating it

---

## 7. Audience / demand topics

Recommended dimensions:
- target audience definition
- key pain points or desires
- willingness to pay or act
- substitute behaviors
- unmet demand
- segmentation differences
- evidence strength

---

## 8. Business model / monetization topics

Recommended dimensions:
- value proposition
- customer willingness to pay
- pricing logic
- cost or delivery burden
- margins or economic pressure points
- scalability limits
- monetization risk
- viability judgment
