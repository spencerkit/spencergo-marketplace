# Depth Standard

## 1. Goal

Prevent the report from stopping at surface-level observations, ceremonial conclusions, or generic advice.

---

## 2. Core rule

Do not stop at “what is happening.”
Push each important section toward:
1. what is happening
2. why it is happening
3. what mechanism drives it
4. what it means for the user's decision
5. what should be done next

---

## 3. Invalid shallow patterns

The following do not count as sufficient analysis:
- restating an industry phenomenon with no deeper mechanism
- giving a general conclusion with no operational implication
- saying “产品要有差异化” or “策略要清晰” without showing how to build them
- giving advice with no case support
- giving a trend statement with no causal explanation

---

## 4. Required depth pattern for important claims

For every important recommendation or analytical point, try to provide:
- claim
- reason / mechanism
- example or case
- operational path

Example structure:
- 判断：为什么重要
- 机制：它为什么会成立
- 案例：别的作品/平台/样本怎么体现
- 操作路径：用户下一步具体怎么做

---

## 5. Case rule

When giving craft advice, include concrete examples whenever possible.
Examples may come from:
- representative works
- platform trends
- structural comparison between works
- common successful pattern and failed pattern contrast

Do not use examples as decoration.
Use them to prove or sharpen the point.

---

## 6. Operational-path rule

Every major recommendation should tell the user what to do next in a concrete sequence.
Bad:
- 产品要有差异化
Good:
- 先定义目标用户最在意的价值点
- 再说明现有替代方案哪里没有满足它
- 再列出准备在哪个环节做出差异化
- 再用一个具体案例说明这种差异如何真正起作用

## 7. Benchmark rule

Before final delivery, compare the report against `report-quality-benchmarks.md`.
If it still looks like a weak report, tighten it before sending.
