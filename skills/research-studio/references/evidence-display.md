# Evidence Display

## 1. Goal

Make evidence strength and missing information visible in the report.

---

## 2. Display rule

For important claims, show source situation and evidence status in a compact, readable way.

Useful labels include:
- 已有明确来源
- 多来源支持
- 单一来源
- 证据较弱
- 待补充

---

## 3. Missing-data handling

If a number or fact is missing:
- show it as blank or 待补充
- do not hide the absence
- do not smooth over it with vague wording

---

## 4. Placement

Evidence notes can appear:
- inline with key findings
- as small source badges
- in a source notes section

Keep them visible but not noisy.
