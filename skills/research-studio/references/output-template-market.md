# Market / Industry Output Template

Use when the topic is mainly about an industry, sector, or market category.

## Default compact structure

### 结论
One-line judgment about the market's current state and whether it is worth attention.

### 核心发现
- market state
- strongest growth driver
- strongest constraint

### 多维度结果
- 市场现状：what stage the market appears to be in
- 需求侧：who wants it and why
- 供给侧 / 竞争：who is already there and how crowded it feels
- 商业化：how money is captured
- 风险：main structural or regulatory or demand-side risk
- 机会：where the real opening still seems to be

### 最大机会
Name one concrete opening.

### 最大风险
Name one concrete risk.

### 信息缺口
What is still unclear.

### 下一步建议
What should be validated next.

## What to emphasize

Emphasize:
- market maturity
- demand drivers
- competitive density
- structural barriers
- realistic opportunity window

Do not turn the report into a pile of industry clichés.
Be concrete.
